The Craft Journey

From Pencil Sketch to Raised Cotton Texture

Mass-produced prints peel and fade. But when Rakshita stitches your story, it starts with an authentic pencil study on tracing paper, calculating thread tension, stitch density, and color harmony.

  • ✓ Free Custom Sketch Review: We verify spellings and proportions with you before taking a single stitch.
  • ✓ Dimensional French Knots: Raised flower buds you can actually feel with your fingertips.
  • ✓ Double-Tied Anchor Knots: Carefully fastened on the back so the embroidery never unravels in the wash.
